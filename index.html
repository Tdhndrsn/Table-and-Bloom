Welcome back 🌸

Table & Bloom is a wellness space for Black women — built around food, herbs, and community, not rules or restriction. This is the table where we gather: to log what we eat, learn what our ancestors knew about herbs and healing, and find each other across cities and zip codes. No shame, no scorekeeping. Just honest care, one day at a time — and your own blooming season, held gently.
